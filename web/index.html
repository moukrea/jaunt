MADE FOR YOUR ACTUAL WORK
Your tools come with you.
A full terminal
Run your everyday shell, development tools, Claude Code or Codex. Nothing limits you to an agent.
A shared workspace
Keep several sessions, split the desktop into panes, and pick up the same workspace as tabs on your phone.
Files and images
Upload, download and paste from the device in your hand. Image delivery clearly shows a path or supported native paste.
Room to move
Saved device keys reconnect when your network changes. New hosts preserve running shells during runtime updates.
Stay informed
Receive program notifications and open the matching session. Native Android and desktop apps are available.
Your own machine
No VPN and no account for your users. Pair with a one-use QR and revoke a device whenever you need.
